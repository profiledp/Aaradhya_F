Design Approval

Customers must check and approve all names, spellings, phone numbers, and photos before printing starts, as we cannot fix customer typos later.

💳

Advance Payment

At least 50% advance payment is required to confirm your order, with the remaining balance due upon pickup or delivery.

🚫

No Cancellations

Because items are custom-made for you, orders cannot be canceled or changed once printing has begun.

🎨

Color Differences

Slight variations between phone screen colors and actual printed colors are normal and are not considered defects.

🛠️

Print Defects

If there is a printing mistake or damage from our side, we will reprint your order free of cost if reported at the time of pickup or within 24 hours.

📦

Pickup & Storage

Please collect your completed order within 7 days of notification, as we cannot be responsible for unclaimed items stored longer.
