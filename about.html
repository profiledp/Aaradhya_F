Who We Are

Welcome to Aaradhya Mart! We are a multi-service store based in Bongariya Bazar, Azamgarh, dedicated to serving our community with the best products and services.

We started with a simple vision: to make everyday shopping easy and accessible for everyone. Today, we proudly operate three distinct verticals:

  • Aaradhya Fashion: Trendy and comfortable clothing for Men, Women, Kids, Sports, and Winter wear.
  • A-Print (By Aaradhya Fashion): High-quality custom printing services including T-shirts, banners, and visiting cards.
  • Aaradhya Kirana Store: Fresh groceries, daily essentials, and household items delivered right to your doorstep.

Order & Shipping Policy

1. How to Place an Order?

Placing an order is very simple. Browse our products, select your size/quantity, and click "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now". Fill in your delivery details and click "Confirm & Send on WhatsApp". Your order will be instantly sent to our team on WhatsApp for confirmation.

2. Delivery Area & Time

Currently, we deliver within Azamgarh city and nearby areas. For local grocery (Kirana) orders, delivery is usually done within 24 hours. For Fashion and Print items, it may take 2-3 days depending on customization.

3. Delivery Charges

Delivery is FREE for orders above ₹500. For orders below ₹500, a nominal delivery charge of ₹30 may apply. Our team will confirm the final amount on WhatsApp.

Return & Refund Policy

1. Fashion (Clothing)

We accept returns/exchanges for clothing only if there is a size issue or manufacturing defect. The item must be unused with tags intact. Please report the issue within 24 hours of delivery via WhatsApp.

2. Kirana (Grocery)

For grocery items, returns are only accepted if the product is expired, damaged, or wrong. Please share a photo of the product on WhatsApp within 12 hours of delivery.

3. A-Print (Custom Items)

Since custom printing is done based on your specific requirements, custom printed items are non-refundable unless there is a printing mistake from our side.

4. Refund Process

Once the return is approved, refunds for online payments will be credited back to your UPI/Bank account within 3-5 business days. For COD orders, we can offer a replacement or store credit.
