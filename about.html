About Aaradhya Mart

Your trusted local store for Fashion, Custom Printing, and Daily Grocery Essentials in Azamgarh.

Who We Are

Welcome to Aaradhya Mart! We are a multi-service store based in Bongariya Bazar, Azamgarh, dedicated to serving our community with the best products and services.

We started with a simple vision: to make everyday shopping easy and accessible for everyone. Today, we proudly operate three distinct verticals:

  • Aaradhya Fashion: Trendy and comfortable clothing for Men, Women, Kids, Sports, and Winter wear.
  • A-Print (By Aaradhya Fashion): High-quality custom printing services including T-shirts, banners, and visiting cards.
  • Aaradhya Kirana Store: Fresh groceries, daily essentials, and household items delivered right to your doorstep.

Order & Payment Policies

1. Advance Payment (20%)

To confirm your order and initiate the packing process, a 20% advance payment is mandatory. This ensures a smooth and secure transaction for both parties.

2. Cancellation Rules

  • Before Packing: Orders can be cancelled free of cost before they are packed.
  • After Packing / Out for Delivery: Orders cannot be cancelled once they are packed or out for delivery.
  • After Delivery (Return): We do NOT have a 7-day return policy. If you cancel or return an order after delivery, delivery charges will be deducted from your refund.

Product Specific Policies

1. Grocery (Kirana)

For health and hygiene reasons, grocery items cannot be returned once delivered. Returns are only accepted if the item is expired, damaged, or wrong.

2. A-Print (Custom Printing)

Custom printed items are made specifically for you and are non-returnable and non-refundable. We only replace them if there is a printing mistake from our side.

3. Fashion (Clothing)

Clothing items can be exchanged for size issues within 24 hours of delivery, provided the tags are intact and the item is unused.

Frequently Asked Questions

🚚 How long does delivery take?

Local delivery in Azamgarh takes 24-48 hours. For outside Azamgarh, it may take 3-5 business days.

📦 What if I receive a damaged product?

Please share an unboxing video or photos on WhatsApp within 12 hours of delivery. We will replace it for FREE.

💳 How can I pay the 20% advance?

Our team will share our UPI ID or QR Code on WhatsApp after you place the order. You can scan and pay instantly.

🔄 How do I get a refund?

Once a return is approved, refunds are processed within 3-5 business days. Delivery charges are non-refundable.
