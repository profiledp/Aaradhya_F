Quick Answers

💰 How do I pay the 20% advance?

After placing your order, our team will send you a UPI ID or QR code on WhatsApp. Scan, pay, and send the screenshot. Order confirms within minutes.

🚚 How long does delivery take?

Azamgarh city: 24–48 hours. Outside Azamgarh: 3–5 business days via courier.

📦 Can I cancel my order?

Yes, before packing is done — FREE of cost. Once packed or out for delivery, cancellation is not allowed. Contact us on WhatsApp immediately if you need to cancel.

🔄 What is your return policy?

We don't have a 7-day return policy. Returns are accepted only for damaged, expired, or wrong items — reported within 12 hours with photos/video. Delivery charges are non-refundable.
