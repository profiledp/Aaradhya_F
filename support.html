Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay the 20% advance payment?

After placing your order, our team will send you a UPI ID or QR code on WhatsApp. Simply scan, pay the 20% advance, and send us the payment screenshot. Your order will be confirmed within 10 minutes.

How long does delivery take?

For Azamgarh city: 24–48 hours. For locations outside Azamgarh: 3–5 business days via courier service. You'll receive a tracking link on WhatsApp once your order is shipped.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, orders can be cancelled FREE of cost before packing. Once the order is packed or out for delivery, cancellation is not allowed. Please contact us on WhatsApp immediately if you need to cancel.

What is your return policy?

We do NOT have a 7-day return policy. Returns are accepted only for damaged, expired, or wrong items — reported within 12 hours of delivery with photos or video. Delivery charges are non-refundable for post-delivery returns.

Are custom printed products returnable?

No, custom printed items (A-Print) are made specifically for you and are non-returnable and non-refundable. We only replace items if there is a printing mistake from our side.

Can I exchange clothing for a different size?

Yes, clothing items can be exchanged for size issues within 24 hours of delivery — provided tags are intact and the item is unused. Contact us on WhatsApp with your Order ID and preferred size.
